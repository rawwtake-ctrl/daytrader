# Trading YouTube Channels & Podcasts — Brutal Honest Filter

**Date compiled:** 2026-05-14
**Audience:** Experienced trader (runs trading bots), limited learning hours, cannot afford to watch random YouTube content
**Method:** Practitioner pedigree > content polish. Verified P&L > "I bought a Lambo." Specific methodology > vague mindset. No course-shilling-as-primary-product.

> **Honest preamble:** The trading YouTube ecosystem is roughly 5% real practitioners, 15% educators with genuine knowledge, 30% snake oil, and 50% entertainment LARPing as education. This document filters ruthlessly. If a channel is in Tier 1 below, the boss can watch with a notebook open. If it's in Section D, he should never click it. The signal-to-noise ratios are estimated honestly based on content review — they are not marketing numbers.

## Section D: SKIP — Content Creators Pretending to Be Traders

If the boss watches any of these, he is losing learning hours. List with brief reasons:

### Hard skips:
- **Warrior Trading / Ross Cameron.** Settled with the FTC in 2022 for $3 million over **false earnings claims** — the vast majority of customers lost money trading after paying for the courses. The FTC literally returned $2.9M to harmed consumers. Prohibited from making baseless earnings claims going forward. <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/04/federal-trade-commission-cracks-down-warrior-trading-misleading-consumers-false-investment-promises>
- **Any "day in the life of a day trader" channel with luxury cars in the thumbnail.** Rented Lambos in Dubai/Turkey Airbnbs is now a known content template. Income source is YouTube + course sales, not trading.
- **Forex YouTube generally.** Survivorship-bias-driven. The combination of high leverage + retail broker conflict of interest + content-creator economics produces an unusually high scam density.
- **TikTok "trading gurus."** Same as above, faster and worse.
- **Anyone promising "X% per week / month" returns.** The math doesn't compound that way for anyone living on Earth.
- **Discord-signal-as-primary-product channels.** Following someone else's signals is not learning to trade and is a known wealth-destroyer.
- **"Prop firm challenge" influencer ecosystem.** Most are affiliate marketers paid per signup. The math of passing a $25K/$100K challenge then trading "funded" capital with 5% drawdown limits is structurally hostile to actual edge.

### Treat with suspicion:
- Channels whose top videos are *all* "I made $X today" thumbnails with no methodology video in the top 10.
- Channels that hide their courses behind a "free webinar" funnel (Russell Brunson playbook).
- Anyone who has never publicly admitted a losing month.

## Section F: The Daily / Weekly Watch Routine

### Morning pre-market (0-2 videos MAX, 5-15 min each):
- **Best option:** FuturesTrader71's "Trader Bite" daily premarket session at 8 AM CT (covers structure, prep, levels). One ~20-min video, sets the tone.
- **Adam Mancini's Substack levels** (paid) for ES traders — read, don't watch.
- **Brian Shannon's premarket video** when he posts (alphatrends.net + YouTube).

### What NOT to watch in the morning:
- **Live trading streams.** They kill more accounts than they help — they create the illusion that you should be trading the same setups someone else is. Your boss runs bots; he doesn't need to mentally chase another human's intraday tape.
- **"Markets today" hype channels.** CNBC-style noise.
- **Twitter doomscrolling.** Use a focused list (Lance, Marci, Mancini, Karsan) — not the full feed.

### Weekly (1-2 longer-form, 60-90 min):
- **Friday:** *The Compound and Friends* (Josh Brown + Michael Batnick) — keeps you current on macro narratives.
- **Sat/Sun:** One Chat with Traders OR Top Traders Unplugged interview.
- **Once a month:** A Damodaran session or Pat Dorsey interview to keep Bucket 3 thinking sharp.

### Quarterly:

## Section I: Trading Podcasts that Beat YouTube for Serious Learning

For a working trader/bot operator, **audio podcasts beat YouTube** for sheer learning ROI per hour because: (a) no thumbnail bait economics distorting content, (b) longer-form (1-3 hour interviews with no editing tricks), (c) you can listen while doing other work, (d) guest density is higher than YouTube — many institutional traders will do a podcast who would never make a YouTube video.

### Tier 1 podcasts (subscribe + work through back catalog):

**1. Chat with Traders (Aaron Fifield, eps 1-243; new hosts thereafter)**
- <https://chatwithtraders.com/>
- 243 episodes under Fifield (2015-2022) covering institutional traders, prop traders, quants, market makers. Back catalog is the single best free trading education resource on the internet.
- **Must-listen episodes:** any episode with Mike Bellafiore, Linda Raschke, Brett Steenbarger, or anyone from Jane Street / Optiver / DRW.

**2. Top Traders Unplugged (Niels Kaastrup-Larsen)**
- <https://www.toptradersunplugged.com/>
- Niels has 35+ years in CTA/trend following, runs DUNN Capital's European + Asian operations.
- Focus: systematic trend following, global macro, commodities, vol. **Perfect for a bot operator.** Weekly.

**3. Capital Allocators (Ted Seides)**
- <https://www.capitalallocators.com/>
- Institutional allocator perspective — how endowments, foundations, pensions, family offices select managers. Different angle on "what makes a real trader" than YouTube ever provides.

**4. Macro Voices (Erik Townsend + Patrick Ceresna)**
- <https://www.macrovoices.com/>
- Weekly, hedge fund manager hosts, A-list macro guests. Best macro podcast for serious traders.

**5. Hidden Forces (Demetri Kofinas)**
- <https://hiddenforces.io/>
- Deeper, slower, more geopolitical. Worth the paywall for the back catalog alone.

**6. The Acquirers Podcast (Tobias Carlisle)**
- <https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-acquirers-podcast/id1454112457>
- 423+ episodes, weekly, ~1 hour each. Deep value, hedge funds, activist investing, special situations.

**7. The Compound and Friends (Josh Brown + Michael Batnick)**
- <https://podcasts.thecompoundnews.com/show/TCAF/>
- Weekly Friday release. Best "current macro narrative" podcast. Not actionable; useful for staying intellectually current with the discourse.

**8. Acquired (Ben Gilbert + David Rosenthal)**
- <https://www.acquired.fm/>
- 3-4 hour deep dives into specific companies. For Bucket 3 / long-term equity thinking. The NVIDIA, Costco, TSMC, LVMH, Standard Oil, and Hermès episodes are masterclasses.

### Honorable mentions:
- **Excess Returns** (Justin Carbonneau + Matt Zeigler) — quantitative + factor investing
- **Bloomberg's Trillions / Odd Lots** — narrow but excellent niche episodes
- **Better System Trader** (Andrew Swanscott) — systematic-trader focused, has had Andrea Unger and many other systematic practitioners on

## Final Honest Verdict

If the boss has **20 hours** to spend on trading content, allocate it like this:

| Allocation | Source | Why |
|------------|--------|-----|
| 6 hours | 20-video starter playlist (Section E) | Foundation |
| 6 hours | Chat with Traders back catalog (cherry-picked) | Best free institutional-trader content |
| 4 hours | One Top Traders Unplugged + one Acquired deep-dive | Systematic + business-quality thinking |
| 2 hours | Damodaran sessions 1-3 | Valuation foundation if any Bucket 3 |
| 2 hours | Brett Steenbarger interviews + traderfeed.blogspot.com | Psychology |

If the boss has **2 hours per week ongoing**, allocate:

- 30 min: One Chat with Traders or Top Traders Unplugged episode
- 30 min: One Lance Breitstein or Marci Silfrain video (new or rewatch)
- 30 min: One Macro Voices or Hidden Forces episode
- 30 min: Reading Steenbarger's traderfeed blog

**What NOT to do:**
- Do not watch live trading streams. They feel productive; they aren't.
- Do not watch "I made $X today" thumbnails. The very economics of those thumbnails predicts noise.
- Do not buy the courses. Every single Tier 1 person in this document either gives the core methodology free (Lance, Brian Shannon's free content, Stockbee's free content) or has a book that's $20 and covers more than the $5,000 course (Hougaard's *Best Loser Wins*, Bellafiore's *One Good Trade* and *The PlayBook*, Pabrai's *Dhandho Investor*, Damodaran's textbooks, Steenbarger's *Trading Psychology 2.0*, Carlisle's *Acquirer's Multiple*, Dorsey's *Little Book*).

**The 80/20 of trading YouTube:** Lance Breitstein + Marci Silfrain + Brett Steenbarger + SMB Capital + Brian Shannon + Damodaran/Dorsey + the top 30 episodes of Chat with Traders ≈ 80% of the educational value of all trading content ever produced. Everything else is rounding error or noise.
