## 1. Her Actual Strategy — "Little RZY" Measured Move Trend Strategy

This is her **published, named** setup (Source 5). She authored a playbook for it on Chart Fanatics and TradeZella. It is the closest thing to a written rulebook in public.

**Core thesis:** "Price moves in repeating, measured patterns within a trend." She trades the **continuation pullback** in established trends, not breakouts and not pure mean-reversion.

**The setup, step by step (verbatim from her playbook):**

1. **Identify the trend on higher timeframes.** Downtrend = lower highs / lower lows. Uptrend = higher highs / higher lows.
2. **Wait for the impulse move AND then a pullback.** Do not enter during the impulse. "The setup forms after the bounce — not during the impulse."
3. **Draw a trendline across the pullback** (across pullback highs in a downtrend, pullback lows in an uptrend). Each Little RZY gets its **own** trendline — it is not one continuous trendline across the whole trend.
4. **Measure the move.** Vertical distance from the swing extreme of the structure to the trendline.
5. **Project that same distance** in the direction of the prevailing trend → that is the profit target.
6. **Entry trigger:** "Wait for the pullback to form and begin rejecting the trendline." Enter on the reversal off the trendline in the direction of the larger trend.

**Pattern reliability rule (important):**
> "First and second Little RZY patterns often produce strong continuation moves. By the fourth or fifth pattern, the move often becomes exhausted."

So she counts how many measured-move structures have already played out in the trend. Early structures = high-probability continuation. Late structures = warn of exhaustion / look for reversal.

**Bollinger Bands — used as context only, not as a signal:**
- Middle band = "normal price" (mean).
- Outer bands = price extension; price beyond outer bands = "out of reality."
- Best longs in an uptrend = when the early Little RZY structures form **near the lower band** (more room to run).
- Best shorts in a downtrend = when early structures form **near the upper band**.
- Price stretched into the opposite extreme band while structures get smaller = trend exhaustion → reversal alert.

**Bollinger period/std-dev settings:** Not specified in her public playbook. **UNVERIFIED** — likely default 20-period, 2 std-dev based on her "keep it simple" ethos, but this is inference.

**Invalidation:** Setup is dead if (a) price closes through and beyond the trendline, (b) the structure becomes irregular/over-extended, or (c) price collapses without a proper pullback.

**Her own honest warning about the strategy:**
> "These patterns are easier to explain in hindsight than they are to catch perfectly in real time. Consistent chart time and repetition are required to improve recognition." (Source 5)

## 3. Risk Management

This is the **single most important** section for your boss. Marci's risk framework is unusually crisp.

**Position sizing (real money, not contest):**
- "Dynamic sizing on winners, static stops on losers." (Source 3)
- She **adds to winners** — but only after the trade is in profit and confirming her thesis. She has said the contest taught her to "trust winners more and scale into them," something she **rarely** did before the contest (Sources 1, 3).
- **No specific %-of-account number is publicly disclosed.** **UNVERIFIED.** Based on her "plan to lose 20 in a row" framing, the implied per-trade risk is small enough that 20 stop-outs in succession would not threaten the account — likely in the 0.25%–1% range per individual entry, but this is inference.

**Stops:**
- **Hard, static stops. NO trailing stops.** (Sources 1, 3 — both summarizers agree.)
- Quote from search result: "Her data indicates it is more profitable for her to either let the trade hit its target or get stopped out completely."
- Stop placement per her playbook: **above the trendline / recent swing high** for shorts, **below the trendline / recent swing low** for longs. She warns against overly tight stops because price commonly retests the trendline before continuing.

**Leverage:**
- The two enemies are **"ego and leverage."** This is one of her most repeated lines.
- Real-money leverage is conservative; she has explicitly disowned her contest leverage as "irresponsible" and not something she would use with her own capital (Sources 1, 3).

**Daily loss limit / max drawdown rules:**
- **No fixed dollar/% daily loss limit is publicly disclosed.** **UNVERIFIED.**
- Her actual circuit-breaker is **behavioral, not numeric**: "If she notices she is becoming emotionally unstable or losing discipline, she stops trading immediately — sometimes for days or weeks." (Sources 1, 3.) This is closer to a tilt-detection rule than a daily loss cap.

**Win rate:**
- "Maintains a win rate **below 50%** while remaining highly profitable." (Source 3.) This is consistent with a trend-continuation strategy where average winners are larger than average losers, and where she scales into winners.

## 5. Mindset and Psychology

This is where she is most quotable. Themes:

She enters every trade *expecting it to lose* and mentally pre-commits to losing the next 20 in a row. The logic: if 20 losses in a row would emotionally destroy you, you are over-leveraged. (Sources 1, 3.)

Repeated across multiple sources. (Sources 1, 3.)

## 8. Common Mistakes She Explicitly Warns Against

4. **Over-leveraging — especially after a loss.** The revenge-trading spiral.

7. **Trying to trade everything.** Stay in your one or two markets where you know the rhythm.

9. **Believing any system works in all conditions.** "No trading system works in all market conditions." Adapt or stand aside.
10. **Underestimating the difficulty.** "About 99% of traders lose money."

## Synthesis — What to Hand Your Boss

If your boss wants a one-page version of her **real-money, day-to-day** method (NOT contest behavior), it is this:

- **Markets:** ES and NQ futures, full stop. Nothing else.
- **Bias engine:** Top-down — monthly → weekly → daily for trend and key levels.
- **Entry setup:** Little RZY measured-move trend-continuation. Identify trend, wait for impulse, wait for pullback, draw trendline on the pullback, measure the swing-to-trendline distance, enter on rejection of the trendline back in the direction of the larger trend, target = measured distance projected.
- **Context filter:** Bollinger Bands — only take early structures (1st/2nd RZY) and prefer them when price is near the band opposite to trend direction (room to run). Avoid late (4th/5th) structures except as reversal signals.
- **Stops:** Hard, static, beyond the trendline / swing. No trailing.
- **Targets:** Measured-move projection. Partials allowed; let the move complete.
- **Sizing:** Dynamic — add to confirmed winners. Conservative base size in real money (specific % not public).
- **Daily kill switch:** Behavioral, not numeric. The instant you feel tilt, walk away. Days or weeks if needed.
- **Routine:** 6 days a week. More hours sim/backtesting than live trading. Personal data collection on every setup. Train across historical regimes.
- **Mental rule:** Pre-commit to 20 losses in a row. If that breaks you, cut size until it doesn't.
- **The two prohibited drugs:** Ego and leverage.

That is the world-class-trader operating system she has been willing to make public. The remaining 20% — exact intraday execution timeframe, exact % per trade, exact Bollinger settings, exact daily-loss number — she has not disclosed and would have to be inferred or filled in by your boss through his own backtesting on ES/NQ.
