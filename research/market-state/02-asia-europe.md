### Asia narrative — what is actually driving the tape
1. **Trump–Xi summit in Beijing is THE trade.** Air Force One landed at Beijing Capital on May 13. Jensen Huang travels with the President; market is pricing a partial Nvidia AI-chip re-opening to China, which is exactly why Korean memory ripped and Taiwan got dumped (winner-loser rotation inside the Asia chip stack). Morgan Stanley flagged a Xi–Trump positive tape outcome could lift Chinese onshore equity 12%.
2. **Bessent / He Lifeng pre-meeting at Incheon (~3 hrs)** wrapped on the economic/trade track — set the floor for the summit, kept HK from breaking lower.
3. **JGB tantrum quietly back.** 10y JGB through 2.6% — first time since 1997. BOJ April Summary of Opinions ("rate hike as early as next meeting") + sticky Tokyo CPI = yen-positive yield differential narrowing slowly but not fast enough to stop USDJPY's drift back to 157+.
4. **China AI capex thesis re-ignited** (Alibaba: triple-digit AI revenue growth; ChiNext + STAR 50 ATH; METiS TechBio IPO raised HK$2.11B in Hong Kong).
5. **Iron ore demand worries** kept the ASX heavy. Property-driven steel demand contraction is structural overhang.

## 5. Geopolitical risk overnight

- **US–China (Trump–Xi, Beijing):** Trump on tarmac at Beijing Capital. Public Trump language: Xi a "great leader," ties "will be better than ever." Xi: major powers "should be partners, not adversaries." Agenda spans **trade, rare-earth export controls, Taiwan, Iran war, AI/chips**. Nvidia CEO Huang on the trip is the single most important market tell — signals possible AI-chip re-opening. Council on Foreign Relations note: Trump's Iran misstep "weakens his hand."
- **Iran / Strait of Hormuz:** Ceasefire described as **"on life support."** US Navy blockading Hormuz, intercepting tankers bound for China. Q1 flows down ~6 mbd. Beijing providing political (and possibly intelligence) support to Tehran — meaning Iran is INSIDE the Xi–Trump conversation, not adjacent to it.
- **Israel / Hezbollah:** Lebanese ceasefire holding but >10,000 homes destroyed/damaged; sporadic Israeli strikes on Beirut still being reported. Not a current market mover.
- **Russia / Ukraine:** May 9–11 Victory Day 3-day ceasefire expired; both sides accused each other of violations. Trump signaled hope for extension. Energy/agri implications quiet for the moment.
- **Taiwan:** Crisis Group does **not** put Taiwan in 2026 top-10 conflicts assuming summit goes ok — but TAIEX −1.71% today tells you the local market hedged anyway. Asymmetric risk.
- **Tariffs / trade:** US–China bilateral trade reported down ~30% during tariff fight; any framework-deal headline out of Beijing is +2–3% S&P trigger; any walk-out is −2% trigger.
