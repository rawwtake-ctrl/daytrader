## 6. Cross-Asset Macro Narrative

**Dominant theme overnight: Stagflation-lite + AI-capex bid**, layered on Iran risk premium.

Five forces clashing:

1. **Inflation reaccelerating** - April CPI 3.8% (a 3-year high), PPI +1.4% m/m (sharpest since 2022). Energy-driven via Iran but core CPI also doubled to 0.4%, hitting services not just tariffs
2. **Fed sidelined / hawkish** - 2026 cuts gone, ~35% pricing a HIKE in December. 10Y to 4.48% (highest since July 2025), 30Y near 5%
3. **AI bid overpowering rates** - SPX/NDX at fresh all-time highs led by NVDA/MU on May 13. Tech is decoupling from the yield curve
4. **Iran/Hormuz premium** - keeping oil bid, gold bid, dollar bid all simultaneously (a rare trinity that signals genuine geopolitical risk pricing rather than just a directional macro view)
5. **China optionality** - Trump-Xi summit hopes propping CNH and copper, while AOJ-style underlying skepticism (property + consumer sentiment weak per Citi outlook). CNH at 6.78 is the tell

**Risk regime:** Not classic risk-on, not classic risk-off. It is **bifurcated**: equities up + gold up + dollar up + yields up + crypto flat/down = **inflation-hedge regime with a tech monopoly bid**. Bonds are the loser. ETH is the loser.
