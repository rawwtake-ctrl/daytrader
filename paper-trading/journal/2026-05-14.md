## 6 · Three Lessons

1. **WHAT WORKED:** Pre-marked plan from a SOFT data scenario → entered the planned setup at the planned level → result matched thesis. **The work pre-open made the trade easy.** Same setup eyeballed at 9:30 without the plan = panic. **Repeat: always pre-mark before open.**

2. **WHAT FAILED:** Setup D was paper-held all the way to stop. **A partial-scale rule at +1R = $3/share gain instead of $3/share loss = $6/share swing.** The 5-layer exit stack from Doc 04 specifically prescribes this. **STOP: holding paper trades to stop without partial-scale rule. START: 50% at +1R for every trade, no exceptions.**

3. **WHAT SURPRISED:** The MSFT trade looked perfect at 10:05 ET ($403 area, going my way) and STILL FAILED by close. A setup looking right at the 30-min mark does NOT mean it ends right at the close. **The exit is the strategy. Entry was fine; the lack of mid-trade rules was the loss.**
